## Scope

The technical specification for interoperability relating to the
operation and traffic management subsystem (TSI OPE) applies to railway
undertakings and infrastructure managers operating on the Union rail
network. It harmonises operating principles and rules so that cross-border
operation does not require train crews to learn divergent national
practices beyond the published national rules.

## Route knowledge and driver competence interface

RUs must ensure drivers possess and maintain route knowledge and rolling
stock knowledge for the services they operate (interfacing with the Train
Drivers Directive 2007/59/EC certification scheme). Procedures must cover
degraded conditions and route learning.

## Train preparation and operation

Requirements cover: train composition and compatibility checks with the
route (route compatibility check before operation), braking performance
calculation (the RU must ensure braking performance is adequate for the
route and consist, using harmonised braked-weight methods), train visibility
(head and tail signal requirements — the rear-of-train signal harmonised
across the EU, with reflective plates accepted per Appendix), departure
checks, and safety-related communications between driver and signaller using
prescribed communication methodology (repeat-back of safety messages,
European Instruction structure).

## Degraded operation and emergencies

Operating rules must address degraded modes (signal passed at danger
procedures, failure of train radio or signalling, working of trains in
emergency), with clear allocation of responsibility between IM and RU.
Occurrences must be reported and shared between the parties concerned.

## Appendices — harmonised rules and glossary

Appendix A contains ERTMS operating principles and rules (referencing the
ERTMS operational rules document). Appendix B lists common operational
principles and rules (e.g., authority for train movement, protection of a
train in an emergency). Appendix C sets the safety-related communications
methodology, and Appendix D covers route compatibility and the route book.
