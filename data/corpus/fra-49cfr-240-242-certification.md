## Part 240 — Locomotive engineer certification

No person may operate a locomotive unless certified by the railroad under an
FRA-approved certification program. Certification requires: eligibility
review (prior safety conduct, motor vehicle driving record for alcohol/drug
offenses), vision and hearing acuity meeting § 240.121 thresholds
(correctable), knowledge testing, and a skills performance test administered
by a designated supervisor of locomotive engineers (DSLE). Certificates are
valid for no more than three years (36 months) and require periodic
monitoring: at least one unannounced operational compliance test and an
annual operational monitoring observation. Specified rule violations
(e.g., passing a stop signal, exceeding speed by 10 mph or more, violating
§ 219 alcohol/drug rules) require revocation for defined periods, with due
process hearing rights.

## Part 242 — Conductor certification

Parallel scheme for conductors: railroads must have an FRA-approved
conductor certification program covering eligibility, vision/hearing acuity,
knowledge testing, and monitoring. Certification is valid for up to three
years. Revocable offenses mirror Part 240 (e.g., occupying main track
without authority, failure to comply with restricted speed). A person may
hold both engineer and conductor certification; territorial qualification
requirements apply when operating over unfamiliar territory.

## Dispute resolution

Both parts provide a petition process to the FRA Operating Crew Review Board
for persons denied certification or whose certification was revoked, with
further appeal to the FRA Administrator and judicial review.
