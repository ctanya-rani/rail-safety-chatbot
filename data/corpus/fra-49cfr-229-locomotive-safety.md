## § 229.7–229.9 Prohibited acts and movement of non-complying locomotives

Using a locomotive that does not comply with Part 229 is prohibited. A
non-complying locomotive may be moved only as a lite locomotive or dead in a
consist for repair, with a tag ("non-complying" or "bad order") on the
control stand describing the defect, and subject to restrictions (e.g., a
locomotive with defective brakes may not haul cars).

## § 229.21 Daily inspection

Each locomotive in use must be inspected at least once during each calendar
day. A written report of the inspection must be made, and any non-complying
conditions must be repaired or the locomotive handled under § 229.9. The
daily inspection record (Form FRA F 6180-49A cab card or electronic
equivalent) must be displayed in the cab.

## § 229.23–229.33 Periodic inspection

Periodic inspections are required at intervals not exceeding 92 days for
most locomotives (368 days for certain locomotives equipped with advanced
microprocessor controls under specified conditions). At each periodic
inspection, specified components (brakes, running gear, electrical systems,
safety appliances) must be inspected and tested, and the inspection recorded
on the cab card. Annual tests cover load meters, air gauges, and event
recorders; main reservoir hydrostatic tests follow the schedule in § 229.31.

## § 229.41–229.140 Safety requirements

Substantive standards cover brakes and draft systems (independent and
automatic brake operation, hand brakes), suspension and running gear (wheel
defect condemning limits, e.g., flange thickness, rim thickness, wheel
cracks), electrical systems (insulation, grounding), cabs and cab equipment
(cab temperature, noise exposure limits per § 229.121, sanitation § 229.137–
229.139), alerters/event recorders (§ 229.135 event recorder required on
lead locomotives operating over 30 mph, with crashworthy memory for newer
units), and auxiliary lights/headlights (§ 229.125 ditch lights required for
trains over 20 mph over public crossings).

## § 229.140 Remote control locomotives

Remote control locomotives must meet additional requirements for operator
control units, including tilt protection and man-down features where
specified by railroad rule.
