## Purpose and structure

The Regulation sets out the safety management system requirements that
railway undertakings (Annex I) and infrastructure managers (Annex II) must
meet to obtain a single safety certificate or safety authorisation under
Directive (EU) 2016/798. The annexes follow a Plan-Do-Check-Act structure
aligned with ISO high-level structure.

## Annex I/II — Context of the organisation and leadership

The organisation must describe the type, extent and area of its operations,
identify safety risks from its activities, and determine interested parties.
Top management must demonstrate leadership and commitment to safety: a
safety policy communicated across the organisation, safety roles and
responsibilities assigned, promotion of a positive safety culture, and
ensuring staff at all levels are consulted (including staff representatives)
on SMS matters.

## Planning — risk management and safety objectives

The organisation must identify and assess operational risks (including
those from interfaces with other RUs/IMs, contractors, and third parties),
apply Regulation (EU) 402/2013 (CSM-RA) for significant changes, plan
actions to address risks, and set measurable safety objectives with plans to
achieve them. Human and organisational factors must be addressed
systematically through a strategy.

## Operation — competence, information, and operational control

Requirements cover: a competence management system (recruitment, training,
assessment, and maintenance of competence, including fitness checks);
management of safety information and communication; configuration control
of safety-related information; integration of human and organisational
factors in operations; management of contractors, partners and suppliers
(controls to ensure contracted activities meet SMS requirements); management
of assets through their life cycle (including maintenance, linkage to ECM
requirements); and emergency preparedness coordinated with public
authorities and other interfacing organisations.

## Performance evaluation and improvement

The organisation must monitor safety performance (applying the CSM on
monitoring, Regulation (EU) No 1078/2012), conduct internal audits,
carry out management reviews, learn from accidents/incidents through
occurrence reporting and investigation, and continually improve the SMS.
Safety culture must be assessed and improved over time.
